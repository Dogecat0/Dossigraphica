## 1. Geographic Profile Summary

Amazon.com, Inc. is an American multinational technology company headquartered in **Seattle, Washington**, with an expansive global footprint. The company operates through three primary segments: **North America**, **International**, and **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**.

- **AWS Infrastructure:** AWS maintains **114 Availability Zones** across **36 geographic regions** worldwide, serving customers in virtually every country.
- **Workforce:** As of Q4 2025, Amazon employs approximately **1.576 million people** globally.
- **Key Global Initiatives:**
  - **Project Kuiper:** A low Earth orbit (LEO) satellite network with **250+ satellites** already in space, targeting commercial service launch in **Q3 2026**.
  - **Amazon Leo Constellation:** Over **20 launches planned in 2026** and more than **30 in 2027** to expand satellite capabilities.
  - **Zoox:** Autonomous vehicle operations with **350,000+ riders** served.
- **Strategic Investments & Acquisitions:** Amazon holds significant stakes in **Anthropic** and **Rivian**, and has completed major acquisitions including **MGM Holdings** and **One Medical**.

This global positioning enables Amazon to serve hundreds of millions of customers across e-commerce, cloud computing, digital streaming, and artificial intelligence markets worldwide.

### Concentration Risk

Amazon exhibits a **high geographic concentration** in North America, which accounts for 57% of total net sales. ## 6. Regulatory Risk

Amazon operates across dozens of jurisdictions with divergent and often conflicting regulatory regimes. The company faces an unprecedented convergence of antitrust enforcement, data privacy litigation, tax disputes, trade sanctions, and sector-specific regulations that collectively represent one of the most significant threat vectors to its business model. Below is a structured assessment of the most material regulatory risks.

### 6.1 United States: FTC Antitrust Lawsuit & State-Level Actions

| Risk Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk Score** | 5 / 5 (Critical) |
| **Jurisdiction** | United States |
| **Status** | Active litigation (filed September 2023) |

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC), joined by multiple state Attorneys General, filed a landmark antitrust lawsuit alleging Amazon holds monopolies in online superstores and marketplace services. This is the most consequential antitrust action against a US technology company in decades. Beyond the FTC action, Amazon faces:

- **Private lawsuits** seeking billions in damages for alleged price fixing and monopolization.
- **State-level actions** from DC, California, and Arizona alleging antitrust violations.
- **Open investigations** in both US and European marketplaces regarding seller practices.

An adverse ruling could force structural remedies including potential divestiture of marketplace and logistics operations, fundamentally altering Amazon's business architecture.

### 6.2 China: US-China Tariff & Trade War Exposure

| Risk Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk Score** | 5 / 5 (Critical) |
| **Jurisdiction** | China / United States |
| **Status** | Ongoing escalation |

China-based sellers account for significant portions of Amazon's third-party seller services and advertising revenues. China-based suppliers provide critical components and finished goods across Amazon's supply chain. Key developments include:

- US tariffs on Chinese imports and retaliatory Chinese tariffs creating substantial cost uncertainty.
- China extended tariffs on critical chemical solvents and sanctioned US firms.
- China threatened to stop buying US semiconductors.
- Amazon's forward guidance explicitly cites tariff and trade policies as a source of substantial uncertainty.

The intertwining of Amazon's commercial dependence on Chinese supply chains with escalating geopolitical tensions creates a structural vulnerability with no near-term resolution.

### 6.3 Luxembourg: GDPR Fine & Tax Disputes

| Risk Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk Score** | 4 / 5 (High) |
| **Jurisdiction** | Luxembourg |
| **Status** | Under appeal / Active dispute |

Amazon Europe Core S.à r.l. faces a €746 million fine imposed by the Luxembourg National Commission for Data Protection (CNPD) for alleged GDPR violations — one of the largest data privacy penalties ever levied. Additionally:

- The Luxembourg tax authority denied the tax basis of certain intangible assets distributed from Luxembourg to the US in 2021.
- The European Commission's state aid decision against Amazon in Luxembourg (~€250 million recovery) remains under appeal.

These disputes highlight the risks of Amazon's complex European corporate structure and the aggressive scrutiny it attracts from EU regulators.

### 6.4 Italy: Competition Authority Fine & Tax Resolution

| Risk Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk Score** | 4 / 5 (High) |
| **Jurisdiction** | Italy |
| **Status** | Fine paid; appeal ongoing |

The Italian Competition Authority (ICA) imposed a €1.13 billion fine on Amazon entities for alleged EU competition rule infringements related to marketplace and logistics practices. Amazon paid the fine and is pursuing an appeal. In Q4 2025, Amazon's operating income included a $1.1 billion charge for resolution of tax disputes associated with its stores business in Italy and settlement of a related lawsuit.

### 6.5 European Union: Digital Markets Act & Competition Investigations

| Risk Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk Score** | 4 / 5 (High) |
| **Jurisdiction** | European Union |
| **Status** | Active investigations |

Amazon faces open investigations under EU competition rules, including in the European marketplace for sellers. The EU's Digital Markets Act (DMA) imposes new obligations on designated gatekeepers, with Amazon squarely in scope. Additional risks include:

- The European Commission's state aid decision against Amazon in Luxembourg under appeal.
- EU alignment with the US on China is not assured, creating potential divergence in sanctions and trade policy.
- The EU has enacted global minimum taxes and proposed taxes on online advertising and marketplace service revenues.

### 6.6 India: Tax Dispute on Cloud Services & Foreign Ownership Restrictions

| Risk Factor | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Risk Score** | 4 / 5 (High) |
| **Jurisdiction** | India |

Amazon is contesting this position in Indian courts, but an adverse resolution could require significant additional tax payments. Separately, India restricts foreign ownership or control of Indian companies involved in online multi-brand retail trading, directly affecting Amazon's operational structure in one of the world's largest consumer markets.
